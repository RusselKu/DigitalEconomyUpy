### Key Findings Across the 5 Assigned Economies (2023)

1. **Netherlands 🇳🇱 (Global Benchmark):** High volume of patents in Class `G05D 7/00` and `A01G 25/16` related to closed-loop greenhouse automation, hydroponic nutrient recycling, and autonomous robotic harvesters.
2. **New Zealand 🇳🇿:** Strong specialization in pasture management telemetry, automated fertigation, and livestock RFID tracking systems (`A01G 25/00`).
3. **Argentina 🇦🇷:** Emerging patent activity in AgTech software algorithms, computer vision for weed identification (`G06V 20/10`), and satellite NDVI analytics platforms.
4. **Mexico 🇲🇽:** Patent applications are predominantly filed by non-residents (multinational corporations). Resident patent activity per million population remains at **8.8 patents/1M**, with limited patenting in proprietary AgTech algorithms.
5. **Kenya 🇰🇪:** Focus on utility models and fintech-integrated AgTech systems (e.g., Pay-As-You-Go solar irrigation pumps connected to *M-Pesa*).
